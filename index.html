Common questions

Things lifters ask
before installing.

Short answers, mirrored from the privacy policy. If something's missing, mail support@repwiser.fit.

Is RepWiser free?
RepWiser is free to download and use for core workout logging, planning, and history. Some advanced features are available via in-app purchase; you'll see them clearly inside the app. There are no ads.
Do I need an Apple Watch?
No. RepWiser is fully usable on iPhone alone. The Apple Watch companion adds wrist-side controls during a session (rest timer, set complete, focused exercise) but is entirely optional.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Workouts, routines, and history live on-device. Cloud sync is opt-in — turn it on if you want to back up or move to a new phone, otherwise nothing leaves your iPhone.
What does it read from Apple Health?
Only what you grant. Sleep, resting heart rate, HRV, and recent activity are read to show readiness signals. RepWiser can also save your completed workouts back to Apple Health when you enable HealthKit writes.
Can I export my workout history?
Yes. Settings has an export option that produces a portable file of your full history. Apple Health writes also keep a parallel copy in the system Health database.
How is my data stored?
Locally on your device by default. Cloud sync (when enabled) goes to a private RepWiser-managed Supabase project. No third-party trackers, no ad networks. Full details are in the privacy policy.

Your training data stays
focused on training.

RepWiser does not sell personal data or use your data for third-party advertising. Optional features such as Apple Health, location routes, progress photos, cloud sync, and AI coaching are used to power app functionality only.
