RepWiser Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

Who we are

RepWiser is developed by Biswajit Bastia ("we", "us", "our"). For privacy questions, contact: your-email@example.com

What we collect

  • Account information: email address and encrypted password, managed via Supabase Auth.
  • Workout data: exercises, sets, reps, weights, and notes you log in the app.
  • HealthKit data (only if you grant permission): heart rate, active energy, workout sessions. This data NEVER leaves your device.

How we use it

  • To save and sync your workouts across your devices via Supabase.
  • To generate AI-powered training suggestions through OpenAI's API. Only anonymized workout summaries are sent — never your name, email, or any personally identifiable information.

Where data is stored

  • Account and workout data: Supabase (hosted on AWS, US region)
  • HealthKit data: stays on your device only, never transmitted

Your rights

You can request deletion of all your data at any time by emailing your-email@example.com. We will delete your data within 30 days of receiving your request.

What we don't do

  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers.

Children's privacy

RepWiser is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if our privacy practices change. Continued use of RepWiser after changes means you accept the updated policy.
